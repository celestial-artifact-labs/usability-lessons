# usability-lessons

Usability lessons learned across products, published in project-free form: the rule, why it holds, how to check it.

## What this repository is

This repository is for publishing general usability lessons, written for people who design, build, or review interfaces and want rules they can check their own work against. Each lesson has three parts:

- **The rule.** A general statement of what to do, or not do, so that people can use what you build.
- **Why it holds.** The reason the rule is true, so you can judge whether it applies to your situation.
- **How to check it.** A step someone else can repeat to find out whether the rule was followed.

"Project-free" means a lesson is written without naming the product, repository, person, brand, screen name, or key binding it was learned from. What is published is the general rule, not the story of where it came from.

The repository is also meant to carry public cases: short, project-free accounts of one usability failure, or of one decision taken to prevent one (what a person was trying to do, what went wrong, why it mattered, and how it ended), that a lesson was learned from.
